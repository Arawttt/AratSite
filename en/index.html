Questions, answered

What working with melooks like.

What kind of projects do you take?

Websites (portfolio, business, store, landing page), admin panels, Telegram bots and mini apps, and AI automation. If it runs on the web or inside Telegram and works with people, it’s in my scope.

How fast can you ship?

It depends on the size of the work; I’ll give you a clear timeline up front and deliver on it — no empty promises, no endless meetings.

What does it cost?

First we look at what you need, then I give you an exact offer and a clear price — before the work starts, not in the middle. Questions get answered before we begin.

Who owns the code and accounts?

Yours — completely. The project runs under your own account and the source code, domain, SSL and access are handed over at delivery. If we ever stop working together, nothing breaks.

Do you provide hosting?

We pick the host together; I’ll tell you exactly what to buy (usually a small server) and I handle the whole setup.

What happens after launch?

Support, updates and fixes — usually the same day. Bigger changes get a clear scope and price before anything moves.

How do we start?

Message me on Telegram or by email with what you want built — one straight conversation about scope, price and timeline, then work starts.
